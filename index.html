About Me

Hello visitors! I’m Elena Viscardi, an Italian physicist who swapped espresso for Helles to pursue a PhD in Astrophysics at the European Southern Observatory (ESO) headquarters in Garching, Germany.
My research focuses on protoplanetary disks, disks of gas and dust that surround young stars. Specifically, I investigate where, when, and how dust grains aggregate into km-sized planetesimals, the rocky precursors to planets.
I primarily work with radio interferometers, big arrays of radio antennas, able to capture the thermal emission of dust grains. But my scientific (and personal) curiosity spans into the infrared and optical regime.
My unofficial hobby? "Collecting" visits to world-class observatories.
